# How to Make Someone Sit in Smoke

| l01 | The Trainer | If a room filled with smoke, would you get up and **leave?** |

| l28 | The Trainer | So how long would you sit in smoke? As long as everyone else does. |

## Sources

- Bibb Latané and John M. Darley, Group inhibition of bystander intervention in emergencies, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1968, volume 10, number 3, pages 215 to 221, https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026570 : people filling in a questionnaire while smoke came into the room. Alone, 75% reported the smoke within the six minutes (three out of four); with two others who had been told to stay passive, 10% did (1 of 10); in groups of three real participants, someone reported it in 38% of groups.
