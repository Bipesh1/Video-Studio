## Sources

- Uri Gneezy and Aldo Rustichini, "A Fine Is a Price", The Journal of Legal Studies, vol. 29, no. 1, January 2000, pages 1 to 17. Ten private daycares in Haifa, Israel, watched for 20 weeks from January to June 1998. At the start of week 5 a fine of NIS 10 per child for coming more than ten minutes after closing (four in the afternoon) was brought in at six of them, chosen at random; late pickups rose over 3 to 4 weeks and settled at almost twice the starting level, and did not come down when the fine was removed at the start of week 17. Nothing changed at the four daycares without a fine. The fine was paid to the owner, not to the teacher who stayed late, who got no extra money. https://rady.ucsd.edu/_files/faculty-research/uri-gneezy/fine.pdf
