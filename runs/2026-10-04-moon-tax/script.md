# The King Who Taxed the Moon

An original short folktale, written by Claude for Biku on 2026-10-04. Script mode: original.
Hand-built short (the full pipeline arrives in Phase 4). Voice only: no music or sound effects yet.

## Script

| Line | Who | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| n1 | Narrator | Long ago, a proud king declared that the **moon** belonged to him. |
| n2 | Narrator | Anyone who walked in its light had to pay him a **coin**. |
| n3 | Narrator | One night, a little girl named Mira stood before him. |
| d1 | Mira | Then the moon owes **me**, king. It followed me all the way home! |
| n4 | Narrator | The king looked up. He could not make the moon **stop**. |
| n5 | Narrator | So the tax ended. And the moon still walks everyone home, for **free**. |

## Shots

1. **The proud king.** Stormy night sky with a crescent moon, the ruler with his scepter, smug. Slow push-in.
2. **The villager.** A tired, sad villager walks through the moonlit town. The camera follows.
3. **Mira and the king.** Mira, small, steps up and points at the moon while she speaks. The king goes from angry to surprised.
4. **Close-up.** The king looks up at the moon, worried, a sweat drop on his head.
5. **Walking home.** Mira walks home happy. The scenery slides past and the moon stays with her.

## Characters

- **The king:** the studio's own ruler costume (crown, crimson cape, steel armor, scepter).
- **Mira:** a small plain stick figure. **The villager:** a plain stick figure.
- Default white heads, since nobody in the tale is a real or specific person.
