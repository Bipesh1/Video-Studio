## Sources

- Bibb Latané and John M. Darley, Group inhibition of bystander intervention in emergencies, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1968, volume 10, number 3, pages 215 to 221, https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026570 : people filling in a questionnaire while smoke came into the room. Alone, 75% reported the smoke within the six minutes; with two others who had been told to stay passive, 10% did (1 of 10); in groups of three real participants, someone reported it in 38% of groups. Fairly sure: the figures were checked on 2026-10-12 against several summaries of the paper that agree with each other and with the citation (a NIST record gives the same journal, volume and pages); the paper itself was not opened.
