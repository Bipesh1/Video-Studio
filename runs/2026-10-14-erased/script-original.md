ERASED

YouTube title: ERASED - An Animated Short Film
Length: 3:00, no dialogue
Logline: When his grandfather starts disappearing line by line, a little boy picks up a pencil and refuses to let him go.

Thumbnail idea (matching the style you shared): Split frame. Left side: Grandpa, half of his body faded to faint grey lines, eraser crumbs falling around him like snow. Right side: the boy, kneeling, pencil in hand, drawing Grandpa’s face back in, warm yellow light behind him. Big bold text across the top: ERASED, with the last three letters half rubbed out.

Two things to set up early (they pay off at the end)
The pencil: Grandpa gives the boy his pencil in the first scene.
The smiling sun: Grandpa always draws the sun with a little smiley face. It’s his signature.
THE SCRIPT
SCENE 1: The Bench (0:00 to 0:35)

Visual: A blank cream-coloured page. A pencil sketches a hill, a tree, a bench. Grandpa appears: hat, round glasses, walking stick, a little belly. Then the boy runs in and jumps onto the bench beside him.

Grandpa raises one finger and draws a circle in the sky. It becomes the sun. He adds two dots and a curve: a smiley face. The sun beams. The boy laughs and tries it himself. His sun is wobbly and lopsided. Grandpa laughs, takes the pencil from behind his ear, and places it in the boy’s hand.

Sound: Soft solo piano. Pencil scratching. Birds. The boy’s giggle.

SCENE 2: The First Crumbs (0:35 to 1:05)

Visual: A normal day at home. Grandpa reaches for his hat on the hook. The brim is missing, just gone, a clean pale gap. He puts it on anyway. Nobody notices.

At dinner, a tiny shower of grey eraser crumbs falls from above. Grandpa’s hand fades for a second and his spoon misses the bowl. He laughs it off. The boy looks down and sees the grey crumbs on the floor. He picks one up. It’s soft. He looks up at the ceiling. Nothing there.

Sound: Piano continues, but one note now sounds slightly off. A faint rubbing sound, like an eraser on paper.

SCENE 3: The Sun Without a Face (1:05 to 1:30)

Visual: Back on the bench. Grandpa lifts his finger to draw the sun like always. He draws the circle... and stops. He stares at it. He can’t remember what comes next. The sun hangs there, blank and empty.

The boy waits. Then he quietly reaches up with the pencil and adds the smile himself. Grandpa looks at it, then at the boy, and pats his head. But his eyes look far away.

Sound: Music drops to a single held note. Silence after the smile is drawn.

SCENE 4: Who Are You? (1:30 to 1:50)

Visual: The boy runs to Grandpa with a drawing. Grandpa turns around, and half of his face is gone. One eye, half a mustache. He looks at the boy and steps back, confused, like he’s meeting a stranger. He holds his walking stick between them.

The boy freezes. The drawing slips from his hand.

Sound: Complete silence. Just one paper rustle as the drawing hits the floor.

SCENE 5: Redraw (1:50 to 2:20)

Visual: The boy grips the pencil. Hands shaking, he steps forward and draws Grandpa’s missing eye back in. It’s too big. He draws the glasses. Crooked. The mustache. Lopsided and silly.

Grandpa blinks. His face softens. He recognises him. He touches the wonky mustache and bursts out laughing. The boy laughs too, tears in his eyes. They hug.

But over Grandpa’s shoulder, the grey crumbs start falling again. Faster. The boy draws faster. A hand. A shoulder. The hat. The pencil gets shorter and shorter with every line.

Sound: The piano theme from Scene 1 returns, fuller now, with strings. Then the rubbing sound grows louder underneath it, fighting the music.

SCENE 6: The Last Line (2:20 to 2:40)

Visual: The crumbs fall like a snowstorm. Grandpa is just a few faint lines now. The boy’s pencil is a tiny stub. He can’t keep up.

Grandpa gently puts his faded hand over the boy’s hand, and the boy stops drawing. Grandpa lifts one finger, the last line he has left, and draws a circle in the sky. Two dots. A smile.

The sun glows. Grandpa smiles at the boy. Then he’s gone. Only his hat drops onto the bench.

Sound: Music cuts. One soft piano note as the hat lands.

SCENE 7: Years Later (2:40 to 3:00)

Visual: Same bench. Same tree. A grown man sits there wearing the old hat, brim still missing. A little girl climbs up beside him. She points at the sky.

He raises his finger and draws a sun. Two dots. A smile. She laughs. He takes a pencil from behind his ear and puts it in her hand.

Camera pulls back. Pinned to the tree is an old childhood drawing: a wobbly stick grandpa with a crooked mustache and glasses too big for his face.

On-screen text (fades in): We are never fully erased. Someone who loves us keeps drawing us back.

Title card: ERASED

Sound: The opening piano theme, gentle and complete. A child’s giggle. Fade out.
