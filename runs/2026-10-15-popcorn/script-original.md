TITLE: Why Does Cinema Popcorn Cost Ten Dollars?
THE QUESTION IN ONE SENTENCE: Why does a bucket of cinema popcorn cost ten dollars?
THE PLACE: Cinema
NEEDS DRAWING: A cinema auditorium: rows of seats facing a lit screen, with a pipe running from the popcorn machine through the wall and puffing a little smell cloud over the seats.

1. THE QUESTION
NARRATOR: Why does a bucket of cinema popcorn cost ten dollars?

[NUMBER: $10 | caption: FOR ONE BUCKET | count up | on: answer]

2. THE MOMENT
NARRATOR: Everyone in the queue pays the ten dollars, because the film is about to start.
[QUEUE: title: EVERYONE PAYS / $10 | 4 people | sign: $10 | on: everyone]
NARRATOR: Most of us assume the cinema makes its money from our *tickets*.
[TAG: title: WHAT WE THINK / PAYS THE BILLS | TICKETS | on: tickets]
NARRATOR: In the US and Canada, the average ticket in 2025 cost about eleven dollars and thirty cents.
[NUMBER: $11.31 | caption: AVERAGE TICKET IN 2025 | count up | on: eleven]

3. FOLLOW THE MONEY
NARRATOR: Your ticket goes to the cinema, and about half of that money goes on to the studio.
[FLOW: title: FOLLOW THE / TICKET MONEY | YOU (on: your) -> $11.31 TICKET (on: ticket) -> THE CINEMA (on: cinema) -> ABOUT HALF (on: half) -> THE FILM STUDIO (on: studio)]
NARRATOR: Of every ticket dollar last year, the world's biggest cinema chain paid studios about forty eight cents.
[NUMBER: 48¢ | caption: TO THE STUDIO, PER $1 | count up | on: forty]
NARRATOR: The studio's cut can be *seventy* percent in the opening week, and about thirty five by week six.
[BARS: title: STUDIO'S SHARE / OF EACH TICKET | OPENING WEEK 70 "70%" (on: opening) | WEEK SIX 35 "35%" (on: six) | red: OPENING WEEK]
NARRATOR: Most tickets sell in those first weeks, when the cinema may keep just thirty cents of each dollar.
[NUMBER: 30¢ | caption: CINEMA KEEPS, OPENING WEEK | count up | on: thirty]

4. THE TRICK
NARRATOR: So the cinema makes its real money where the studio gets *nothing*: the snack counter.
[SCENE: cinema counter | title: WHERE THE CINEMA / REALLY EARNS | show: machine (on: snack), bucket (on: counter)]
NARRATOR: One cinema owner said the corn costs about eight cents and the tub ninety two, so a dollar in all.
[RECEIPT: title: WHAT GOES IN | CORN 8¢ (on: corn) | TUB 92¢ (on: tub) | TOTAL $1]
NARRATOR: Of every snack dollar, the food and cups cost about eighteen cents, and the cinema *keeps* eighty two.
[SPLIT: title: EVERY SNACK / DOLLAR | FOOD AND CUPS 18¢ 18% (on: food) | THE CINEMA KEEPS 82¢ 82% (on: keeps)]
NARRATOR: That same owner even pipes the popcorn *smell* into the room where the film plays.
[SCENE: cinema auditorium | title: THE SMELL IS / PART OF THE PLAN | show: pipe (on: pipes), smell cloud (on: smell), screen (on: film)]
NARRATOR: In opening week, the cinema keeps about eight dollars twenty from a bucket, but three forty from a ticket.
[BARS: title: OPENING WEEK: / WHAT THE CINEMA KEEPS | ONE BUCKET 8.20 "$8.20" (on: bucket) | ONE TICKET 3.40 "$3.40" (on: ticket) | red: ONE BUCKET]

5. THE PROOF
NARRATOR: In 2025, the average visitor to America's biggest chain spent about eight dollars and fifty seven cents on snacks.
[NUMBER: $8.57 | caption: SNACKS PER VISITOR, 2025 | count up | on: eight]
NARRATOR: You meet the same trick at home, where the printer is cheap and the ink is not.
[TAG: title: SAME TRICK / AT HOME | CHEAP PRINTER | note: PRICEY INK | on: printer]

6. YOUR MOVE
NARRATOR: So *share* one bucket between two, and it costs you five dollars each.
[TAG: title: SHARE IT | $5 | note: EACH, SHARED | was $10 (on: costs) | on: share]
NARRATOR: Because the ticket pays for the movie, but the popcorn pays for the cinema.
[NUMBER: $10 | caption: PAYS FOR THE CINEMA | on: popcorn]

- ABC News, "What Does a Trip to the Movies Really Cost You?" (2014). A California cinema owner says the corn for a large bag costs about 8 cents, a tub about 92 cents with the corn, and that he pipes the popcorn smell into the auditorium. https://abcnews.go.com/blogs/lifestyle/2014/03/what-does-a-trip-to-the-movies-really-cost-you-2/
- KTLA (via KRQE), "$70 for a popcorn bucket? Movie theaters are seeing big business with blockbuster collectables" (2026). A large bucket costs a cinema under $2 to serve and sells for about $10. https://www.krqe.com/entertainment-news/70-for-a-popcorn-bucket-movie-theaters-are-seeing-big-business-with-blockbuster-collectables/amp/
- The Numbers, Domestic Theatrical Market Summary for 2025. Average ticket price in the US and Canada was $11.31. https://www.the-numbers.com/market/2025/summary
