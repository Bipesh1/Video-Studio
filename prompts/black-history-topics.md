# Black history page: finding stories

## Story finder (v1.0, 2026-10-24)

For Biku: paste everything under the line into an AI that can search the web, list the page's earlier videos at the bottom, and it gives you ten stories to choose from, each scored. Only for the Black history page.

You are finding stories for a Facebook page of short animated Reels about Black history: about 75 to 90 seconds each, one true story told from inside it, drawn with dignity, researched from official sources.

## What makes a good story here

1. **It is true and documented.** Two good sources (the National Park Service, the Library of Congress, the National Archives, the Smithsonian, a university or museum) tell it. No legend as fact.
2. **It has one moment you can see.** A man in a captain's hat at a warship's wheel; a woman taking off in a plane no school would teach her to fly; a mask going into a smoke-filled tunnel. The first three seconds are that moment.
3. **A stranger has not heard it, or not this part of it.** A famous name is fine with an angle most people do not know.
4. **The person acts.** Courage, skill, invention, leadership: they are the hero of the story, not only someone it happened to.
5. **It can be told in about 200 words** without leaving out what makes it true.
6. **It leaves a question people want to answer** in the comments, or a fact they want to share.
7. **It can be drawn with dignity** and without showing suffering in detail.

Spread the page across kinds of story, and do not repeat the last few: escapes and rescues; firsts; inventors and scientists; rulers, scholars and cities of Africa; soldiers and sailors; builders of towns, schools and businesses; artists, writers and athletes; ordinary people on one extraordinary day. Across times and places too: ancient and medieval Africa, the Atlantic world, the Americas, Europe, today.

## For each of the ten, give

- **The story in one sentence**, and **the moment** (the first picture).
- **The first line** (at most 12 plain words).
- **Why a stranger watches** (one sentence).
- **Sources:** two, with links only if certain; anything disputed, said so.
- **The world to draw:** the place, the time, the light.
- **Scores from 1 to 5:** the moment, the surprise, true and documented, drawable with dignity, the comments it will get. Add them up.
