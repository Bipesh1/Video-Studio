Why does cinema popcorn cost nine dollars? Why does every screen ask you for a tip? How can the water in your glass be older than the Sun?

How long would your hair grow if you never cut it? The questions are wild; the answers are real science.

Every fact comes from a real source, named under each video.

Subscribe, and you'll never look at an ordinary thing the same way again.
