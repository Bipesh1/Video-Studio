# Quote format playbook

One saying, given a short scene that makes it land. Pick it for a proverb, a line from a speech or a piece of advice. The binding numbers are in `formats/quote.json`; this playbook says how to use the format well. Read it before every quote video. Lessons from Biku's feedback are added here by `/review`; keep it under 80 lines.

## The beats, in order

- **setup**, about 30% of the video. A moment that makes the viewer need the saying: who is speaking, or the trouble it answers. Music: reflective, curious, sorrow. Shots: wide, medium.
- **the-quote**, about 45% of the video. The saying itself, word for word, with time to read it. Music: reflective, hope, calm. Shots: close-up, medium.
- **meaning** (may be left out), about 25% of the video. One line on what it means for the viewer today. Music: hope, calm, reflective. Shots: wide, medium.

## Numbers

- Length: 12 to 45 seconds. A shot stays 2 to 5 seconds.
- Pace: 120 to 150 words a minute with pauses counted. A new question, turn or danger every 10 to 20 seconds.
- Pauses: 600 ms after a line, 1200 ms after a big moment.
- Sound effects: low, 2 to 6 a minute.

## Craft

- The setup makes the viewer need the saying before they hear it.
- Speak the quote slowly, word for word, and leave a long pause after it.
- One place and one character are enough. The face does the work.
- If nobody knows who said it, say so. Never pin it on a famous name.

## Facts

- This is a factual format: a script with no saved sources is refused.
- The wording of the quote and who said it are checked against a saved source.
- When nobody knows who first said it, the video says so and names no one.
