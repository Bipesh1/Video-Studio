# News short format playbook

What happened, why it matters and what comes next, told plainly. Pick it for a recent event. The binding numbers are in `formats/news-short.json`; this playbook says how to use the format well. Read it before every news-short video. Lessons from Biku's feedback are added here by `/review`; keep it under 80 lines.

## The beats, in order

- **headline**, about 15% of the video. The one thing that happened, in a sentence, with its date or place. Music: tense, curious. Shots: medium, close-up.
- **what-happened**, about 40% of the video. The events in order, each with its own picture. Music: tense, curious, sorrow. Shots: wide, medium.
- **why-it-matters**, about 25% of the video. Who is affected and how. Music: reflective, tense, hope. Shots: medium, close-up.
- **what-next**, about 20% of the video. What is expected to happen next, or what is still unknown. Music: reflective, curious, hope. Shots: wide, medium.

## Numbers

- Length: 30 to 90 seconds. A shot stays 2 to 4 seconds.
- Pace: 150 to 175 words a minute with pauses counted. A new question, turn or danger every 20 to 30 seconds.
- Pauses: 400 ms after a line, 700 ms after a big moment.
- Sound effects: low, 3 to 8 a minute.

## Craft

- The first line is what happened, with where or when. No scene-setting.
- Events in the order they happened, each dated.
- Say who is affected in terms of people, not systems.
- End on what happens next, or on what is still not known.
- Real people are plain stick figures with no likeness.

## Facts

- This is a factual format: a script with no saved sources is refused.
- Every event has its date and comes from a saved source, with the date the source was read.
- Opinion is never spoken as fact; a claim someone made is said to be their claim.
- Names are spelled as in the source.
- Real people are drawn as plain stick figures, never with a likeness.
