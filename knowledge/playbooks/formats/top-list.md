# Top list format playbook

A countdown or a list of things, each with its own short scene. Pick it for rankings, records and collections of facts. The binding numbers are in `formats/top-list.json`; this playbook says how to use the format well. Read it before every top-list video. Lessons from Biku's feedback are added here by `/review`; keep it under 80 lines.

## The beats, in order

- **hook**, about 8% of the video. Promise the list and tease the best item on it. Music: upbeat, curious. Shots: medium, close-up.
- **items**, about 62% of the video. One scene for each item, numbered on screen, each one a small surprise. Use this beat once per item. Music: upbeat, curious, comic, tense. Shots: medium, wide, close-up.
- **top-item**, about 20% of the video. The best one, given the most time and the strongest picture. Music: triumph, tense, upbeat. Shots: close-up, medium.
- **sign-off**, about 10% of the video. One line that sums up the list or asks which the viewer would pick. Music: upbeat, reflective. Shots: wide, medium.

## Numbers

- Length: 45 to 180 seconds. A shot stays 2 to 4 seconds.
- Pace: 150 to 175 words a minute with pauses counted. A new question, turn or danger every 15 to 25 seconds.
- Pauses: 350 ms after a line, 700 ms after a big moment.
- Sound effects: high, 10 to 20 a minute.

## Craft

- Count down, so the best is last and the viewer stays for it.
- Every item gets a number on screen as a label, and a pop as it lands.
- Each item is one scene with one clear picture and one surprising detail.
- Give the top item about twice the time of the others.
- Keep the items alike in shape so the list has a rhythm.

## Facts

- This is a factual format: a script with no saved sources is refused.
- Every item and every figure comes from a saved source.
- The order of a ranking follows the source, or the video says the order is its own choice.
