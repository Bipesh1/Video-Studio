# Promo format playbook

A short video that gets the viewer to do one thing: follow, visit, buy or come along. Pick it to promote a page, a product or an event. The binding numbers are in `formats/promo.json`; this playbook says how to use the format well. Read it before every promo video. Lessons from Biku's feedback are added here by `/review`; keep it under 80 lines.

## The beats, in order

- **hook**, about 20% of the video. Show the problem or the wish the viewer already has. Music: curious, comic, tense. Shots: close-up, medium.
- **offer**, about 40% of the video. Show what answers it, in use, with one clear benefit. Music: upbeat, hope. Shots: medium, wide.
- **proof** (may be left out), about 20% of the video. One reason to believe it: a number, a name or a result. Music: upbeat, triumph. Shots: medium, close-up.
- **call-to-action**, about 20% of the video. Say the one thing to do next, and show it on screen. Music: upbeat, triumph, hope. Shots: medium, wide.

## Numbers

- Length: 12 to 45 seconds. A shot stays 2 to 4 seconds.
- Pace: 150 to 175 words a minute with pauses counted. A new question, turn or danger every 10 to 15 seconds.
- Pauses: 300 ms after a line, 600 ms after a big moment.
- Sound effects: medium, 6 to 14 a minute.

## Craft

- Open on the viewer's own problem or wish, never on the product's name.
- Show the thing in use by a character, with one benefit, not a list.
- One reason to believe, in a number or a name Biku supplied.
- End on the one thing to do, spoken and shown as a label.

## Facts

- Everything said about a product, a price, an offer or a date comes from what Biku supplies. Nothing is invented.
