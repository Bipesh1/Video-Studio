## What the research says

- **The subject can be huge.** Veritasium's explainer of the prisoner's dilemma, "What The Prisoner's Dilemma Reveals About Life, The Universe, and Everything" (December 2023), has about 23 million views ([Veritasium](https://veritasium.com/videos/2024/1/15/what-the-prisoners-dilemma-reveals-about-life-the-universe-and-everything), [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/episodes/2777702/veritasium-2023-12-23-what-the-prisoners-dilemma-reveals-about-life-the-universe-and-everything)). It is built on real things: Axelrod's computer tournaments, a real truce in the First World War, impalas grooming each other, and one plain rule at the end (be nice, forgiving, retaliatory and clear).
- **Watching the game played beats being told about it.** Primer explains evolution and game theory with cartoon blobs in simulations; seeing a population change generation by generation "is a powerful way to make sense of a complex concept" ([Kottke](https://www.kottke.org/19/07/simulating-natural-selection)); its videos reach millions ([rosetta.to](https://rosetta.to/u/primerblobs)). Nicky Case's _The Evolution of Trust_ teaches the same ideas with simple art, one message per part, and repeated games played out in front of you ([Exploring Economics](https://www.exploring-economics.org/en/discover/the-evolution-of-trust/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/its-time-for-some-real-game-theory)).
- **Behaviour is one of Kurzgesagt's strongest subjects.** Their loneliness video (2019, about 28 million views by one count, two years in the making) explains a feeling as a biological signal, then shows the trap it builds, then the way out ([Kurzgesagt](https://kurzgesagt.org/projects/loneliness), [rosetta.to](https://rosetta.to/u/kurzgesagt/loneliness)); their addiction video turns on one real experiment, the rats in the cage ([Kottke](https://kottke.org/15/10/are-we-thinking-about-addiction-all-wrong)).
- Animated psychology channels (Psych2Go, Practical Psychology, Better Than Yesterday) reach millions of subscribers with studies made relatable ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/psych2go), [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/practicalpsychologytips/)).

## The direction

1. **The promise:** the hidden rules of why people (and animals, and cells) do what they do.
2. **True, not invented.** Every video is a real event, a real experiment or a real pattern in nature, with sources. The mystery stays (Biku's rule: open on the strangest moment), but it is a true one: soldiers who stopped shooting, a traffic jam from nowhere, people throwing away free money.
3. **Show it played.** The cast (the Cheat, the Saint, the Copycat, the Trainer) play the rule out, often hundreds of them at once in a drawn arena, so the viewer watches the rule work, the way Primer and Nicky Case do. This is the channel's own signature, and nothing a stock-footage channel can copy.
4. **Clear before clever** stays: one idea per video, every step laid out, plain words, no "game theory" jargon.
5. **The mirror:** every video ends where the viewer lives: their friends, family, work, city.
6. **No business or prices** (that is What's the Catch?).
